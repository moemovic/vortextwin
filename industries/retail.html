Retail decisions get made at headquarters but play out across hundreds of stores that never look quite the same.

VortexTwin closes that gap with accurate 3D digital twins of every location—from pop-ups to flagships—so teams can plan resets, verify merchandising, and manage facilities without a plane ticket for every question. Capabilities

What a digital twin does for your stores.

Full visibility into every fixture and square foot, at scale.

01 / 05

Full visibility across every location.

See any store without visiting it. Accurate twins let regional and corporate teams walk each location remotely, compare sites, and make decisions from the same reliable picture—no matter how large the footprint.

02 / 05

Merchandising and planogram compliance.

Verify that stores are set the way they were designed. Capture store sets to check fixture placement, visual merchandising, and planogram compliance across locations—catching drift without sending a field team everywhere.

03 / 05

Store planning and remodels.

Plan resets and remodels against real dimensions. Measure on demand and share accurate layouts with designers and contractors, reducing site visits and keeping projects on schedule.

04 / 05

New-store rollouts and brand standards.

Scale your concept consistently. Use twins to document prototype stores, communicate standards to new locations, and keep every build aligned to the brand—so the tenth store looks like the first.

05 / 05

Training and onboarding at scale.

Standardize the baseline for every new hire. Guided walkthroughs give staff a consistent orientation to store layout, safety, and operations—reducing the variability that comes with hundreds of managers training on their own.
